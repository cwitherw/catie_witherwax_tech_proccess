STRETCH SQUISH SLIP

This project began with a container of cherry yogurt. By stretching, squashing, thickening, dripping, and separating, I arrived at the final product of the neon sign for a strip club on Saturn. Yogurt is alive, made of billions of bacterial cultures, and I aimed to reflect that in the activity of my shapes. Through this process based undertaking, I expanded my horizons of making.

Press and hold to gather the cultures.
